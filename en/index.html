Sell, Trade, and CPM2 transactions

List cars for Sell, offer a Trade, and find buyers in the community. For brokered deals, an administrator confirms receipt of the car in game and credits internal CPM2 units to the seller.

Cars are transferred in the game

The buyer and seller agree on the terms, then transfer the car manually in Car Parking Multiplayer 2.

The CPM2 balance records in-game transactions on this site. It is not real money and does not transfer cars automatically.
